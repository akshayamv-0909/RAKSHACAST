# Privacy Policy for RakshaCast
**Effective Date: September 2026**  
**Package Name: com.rakshacast.app**  
**Organization: RakshaCast Initiative (SIH26077 - Ministry of Earth Sciences Partner)**

---

## 1. Information We Collect
RakshaCast is designed with **privacy-by-design** principles:
* **Precise & Coarse Location**: Collected only while the app is in active foreground use to determine hyper-local severe weather nowcast warnings, flood depth calculations, and safe high-ground evacuation paths. We do **not** perform background continuous location tracking unless an explicit SOS distress broadcast is active.
* **Emergency Distress Signals (SOS)**: If you broadcast an SOS, we collect your reported emergency category, trapped persons count, and GPS coordinates to display in the tactical triage queue for rescue teams.
* **Device Telemetry**: Basic device model and connectivity status to ensure offline Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh packet relay functionality.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data
* To deliver real-time Doppler radar nowcasting and flood early warnings.
* To compute elevation-aware evacuation routes away from submerged bottlenecks.
* To transmit distress beacons to authorized emergency response units.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Sharing
* We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize any citizen data.
* Location data is cached locally on your device and purged after 24 hours.
* Emergency distress data is transmitted solely to authorized disaster management entities (NDRF, SDRF, SDMA).
